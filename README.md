# Ruby Darkrose Light Show

A gift page: Ruby Darkrose's SoundCloud tracks with a rose-themed light show.

## Editing
Open `index.html` and find the `CONFIG` block near the top of the script:
- `soundcloudUrl` – her SoundCloud profile (plays all her tracks) or a playlist link
- `dedication` – the message on the start screen and at the top of the page

Commit and push, and Vercel updates the live link automatically.

## Notes
- The SoundCloud player won't load in previews or from a local file; it only works on the hosted link.
- "Sync to sound" makes the lights follow the music through the mic (best on a laptop).
